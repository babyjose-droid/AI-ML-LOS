Events of default

Each of the following is an event of default: failure to pay any amount when due; any statement or document given to the Lender being false or misleading; use of the loan for a purpose other than {{app.purpose}}; insolvency of the Borrower; or the security, if any, being lost or its value falling materially. On an event of default the Lender may, after giving notice, recall the outstanding amount and enforce its rights under this agreement and the law.
