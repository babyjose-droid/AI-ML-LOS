Prepayment and foreclosure

The Borrower may prepay the loan in part or in full after giving notice. Charges, if any, are as stated in the Key Fact Statement; no foreclosure charge applies to floating-rate loans to individuals for purposes other than business. Within the cooling-off period of {{loan.coolingOffDays}} days the Borrower may exit the loan by paying the principal and the proportionate APR, without any penalty.
